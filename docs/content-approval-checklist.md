# Public Content Approval Checklist

The website intentionally omits the following claims until Gamma Pegassi provides approval-safe evidence.

## Game-development technology

For each engine, language, content tool, testing tool, or platform proposed for publication, provide:

- the technology name and version range where relevant;
- whether the experience belongs to Gamma Pegassi or an individual team member;
- the contributor's role and approximate period of use;
- a project or work sample that supports the experience;
- confirmation that the project name and any visual assets may be published.

Candidate technologies such as Unity, C#, Unreal Engine, C++, Blueprints, Godot, GDScript, and Blender are not current website claims.

## Individual team projects

For every named project, confirm:

- project name and current public status;
- contributor's actual role and responsibilities;
- whether it was independent work or work for a previous organization;
- technologies used;
- approved public link;
- written permission to display screenshots, logos, footage, or store artwork.

## Embedded systems

Before adding embedded systems as a Gamma Pegassi service, confirm:

- approved service scope and practical deliverables;
- team or company experience supporting the claim;
- supported platforms, toolchains, and integration types;
- boundaries around hardware design, manufacturing, electrical engineering, safety, and certification;
- approval-safe examples or documentation.

Until those items are confirmed, the public site does not advertise embedded-systems services.
